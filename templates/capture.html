Privacy & control

  • Only messages from bank-style sender IDs (such as VM-HDFCBK) that look like a payment are sent to your server. Personal chats, OTPs and promotions never leave the phone.
  • UPI notifications are read only from payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and similar), never from chat apps.
